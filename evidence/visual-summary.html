What was demonstrated

  • External agent connected to a real UI Test Actions session and controlled the Android emulator.
  • App install, launch, semantic click, Unicode text, scroll, Back/Home, screenshot, and UI tree all worked.
  • rootAVD patched the Android 15 ramdisk; Magisk 31.1 booted and su -c id returned UID 0 after two cold boots.
  • The guest was isolated from outbound IP traffic before the patch.

What remains open

  • Genuine Play Integrity labels need a signed-in, organization-approved Play Store account or an authorized test app/backend.
  • Magisk 31.1's app package and modules did not survive a cold boot on this image.
  • AlwaysStrong was not executed; its x86_64 PIF Zygisk payload is missing.
  • Two later stock boots stalled at ADB authorization, a headless-runner reliability gap.
